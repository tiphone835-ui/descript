5. "SADMANS" Sick-Day Rules & Acute Illness Precautions

During acute fevers, gastroenteritis (vomiting/diarrhea), or extreme summer heatwaves:

  • HOLD Chlorthalidone and Telmisartan immediately to avoid prerenal acute kidney shutdown and blackout syncope upon rising.

Clinical Deprescribing Guidelines Reference

Key methodologies, frameworks, and consensus criteria governing safe de-escalation

1

UWA / RACGP Consensus (Sept 2025)

*Deprescribing in Older People: A clinical practice guideline* (299 pages, Western Australia Centre for Health and Ageing). Highlights that of 46 clinical areas, zero had Evidence-Based Recommendations (EBRs) due to lack of randomized trials, relying on Delphi Consensus-Based Recommendations (CBRs) and Good Practice Statements (GPS).

2

The 5Ms Geriatric Framework

Standard clinical structure: Mind (sedatives, delirium), Mobility (TUG $>12\text{s}$, gait ataxia), Medications (FRIDs, Beers/STOPP), Multi-complexity (CKD + HF + Diabetes conflicts), and Matters Most (quality of life vs. tight laboratory goals).

3

Indian Regulatory & CDSCO Nuances (2026)

High prevalence of unregulated Fixed-Dose Combinations (FDCs) like Pan-L (Levosulpiride) causing drug-induced parkinsonism, widespread OTC self-medication, tropical heatwave dehydration inducing acute orthostasis, and low-sodium salt substitutes triggering life-threatening hyperkalemia.

4

Sick Day Rules (The SADMANS Protocol)

Temporary withholding checklist during acute fevers, gastroenteritis, or heat waves: SGLT2i, ACEi, Diuretics, Metformin, ARBs, NSAIDs, and Sulfonylureas to prevent acute renal failure and sudden circulatory collapse.

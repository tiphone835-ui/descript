2. Why This Regimen is Harmful

Deep Pathology
Prescribing Cascade Unmasked
Dyspepsia → Pan-L (Levosulpiride) prescribed
↓ Levosulpiride blocks dopamine → Shuffling gait & tremors
↓ Misdiagnosed as "age tremor" → Accidental fall occurred
↓ Tramadol + Alprazolam added for pain & sleep anxiety

Deprescribing Key: Ceasing Pan-L removes dopamine blockade at the root, allowing tremors and unsteadiness to reverse within 4–8 weeks.

Safe Substitutions
"SADMANS" Sick-Day & Dehydration Rules

If the patient experiences fever, diarrhea, vomiting, or extreme heatwave exposure:

  • HOLD Chlorthalidone and Telmisartan immediately to prevent prerenal acute kidney failure and blackout syncope.
  • Hold Glimepiride if fluid/food intake drops below 50%.
  • Resume only when well-hydrated and oral intake normalizes for > 48 hours.
